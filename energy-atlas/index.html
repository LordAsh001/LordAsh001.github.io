Key policy context

  • Electricity Act 2023 lets states license and regulate their own electricity markets; NERC has been transferring oversight state by state.
  • Energy Transition Plan sets a net-zero target for 2060, with gas as a transition fuel.
  • Petroleum Industry Act 2021 created NUPRC (upstream) and NMDPRA (mid/downstream) and commercialised NNPC Ltd.
  • Decade of Gas and the AKK pipeline aim to extend gas to the north for power and industry.
  • Off-grid push: the Rural Electrification Agency's Nigeria Electrification Project and DARES programme fund solar mini-grids and home systems.

About this atlas

The Nigeria Energy Atlas brings Nigeria's renewable and fossil energy resources, the infrastructure built on them, and state-level potential into one open, map-based reference for students, researchers, planners and policy discussion. It sits alongside the Nigeria Forest Reserves, Water Resources and Market atlases.

Method

  • Assets were compiled from public operator, government and news sources. Coordinates are approximate site locations (typically within a few kilometres) and markers for basins, programmes and provinces are symbolic.
  • Capacities are nameplate figures. Available capacity is often much lower because of gas supply, transmission and maintenance constraints.
  • Pipelines are schematic routes, not surveyed alignments.
  • State solar bands are indicative, derived from the documented north–south irradiance gradient; wind classes are qualitative.
  • Regulator status reflects NERC transfer orders reported up to August 2025; check NERC for the latest position.
  • Boundaries: state outlines derived from Natural Earth (public domain) via the Highcharts map collection, simplified for the web.
