Privacy notice: community price agents

Nigeria Market Atlas · Price agents tab

This notice covers the price-agent accounts of the Nigeria Market Atlas. Price-agent accounts and posts are kept in the Market Atlas's own database. They are not shared with, and cannot be seen from, any other tool or website.

What we keep

  • Account: your email address and your password. The password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form that nobody can read. Your email is used only to sign you in and to send sign-in and password-reset codes. It is never shown on the atlas.
  • Public profile: the name, market, state, LGA, short bio and optional LinkedIn/Facebook links you enter. These are shown on your public agent page.
  • Posts: each price you post (item, unit, price, market and date), with your agent name. Posts are published on the atlas and shared as open data under CC BY 4.0, so others may reuse them with credit.
  • Checks: if a price looks unusual it is held until the atlas editor checks it. Only you and the editor see held prices.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and remove any of your posts at any time from your agent page.
  • You can ask for your account and all your posts to be deleted, or for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Posting is voluntary. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Please post only prices you saw or paid yourself, and do not include personal details about anyone in a note.

Where it is stored

Accounts and posts are held by our database provider, Supabase, which may store them outside Nigeria and Canada under safeguards comparable to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the GDPR. Personal data are never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Contact

To ask a question or make a request, email amool@unbc.ca. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
