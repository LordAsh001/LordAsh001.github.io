Good to know

  • Meetings are on Microsoft Teams; the link comes with your confirmation email. In-person meetings at UNBC in Prince George, BC, are possible on request.
  • Bookings open on weekdays, 9:00 to 17:00 Pacific Time, at least two days ahead and up to 90 days ahead.
  • You will get a confirmation and a reminder the day before. You can cancel or change the time from the confirmation email.
  • No time that suits you? Email amool@unbc.ca.

Bookings are handled by Microsoft Bookings through the University of Northern British Columbia. The name, email address and notes you enter are used only to arrange the meeting. See the privacy notice.
