Mode
How the Dojo reads your writing
  1. It is a style and structure coach, not a grammar checker. It flags vague, clichéd or evidence-free sentences, and a few mechanical slips (missing capitals, repeated words, stray spaces). Run a spell-check as well before you submit.
  2. Colours rate each sentence. Green = specific and evidenced (numbers, names, action verbs); blue = sound; amber = vague, hedged or long; red = cliché or no substance. Hover or tap any sentence to see exactly why.
  3. Moves are the sections panels expect. Each lights up in the left column when the Dojo detects it. Guided build walks you through them one at a time.
  4. Meters sum it up. Specificity (evidence), Structure (moves covered), Clarity (length, passive voice, filler), Originality (clichés), Length (against the usual word limit).
  5. You do the rewriting. Nothing is changed for you — edit your draft until sentences turn green. Write in your own words: funders check for copied and AI-generated essays.
