2. Maps and boundaries

  • The atlases are thematic maps for education and research. They are not official or cadastral surveys, were not produced by a registered surveyor, and are not issued or approved by the Office of the Surveyor-General of the Federation or any state survey office.
  • International, state, LGA, reserve, basin and other boundaries come from open datasets (for example geoBoundaries, the World Database on Protected Areas, OpenStreetMap and HydroBASINS). They are approximate, simplified for display, and carry no authority. They do not express any view on the legal status of any territory or on any boundary dispute.
  • Points marked as approximate may be several kilometres from the real site.
  • Do not use these maps for land transactions, boundary or ownership questions, legal proceedings, navigation, engineering design or safety decisions. For authoritative maps, contact the Office of the Surveyor-General of the Federation or the relevant state Surveyor-General.

3. Data, prices and alerts

  • Data are compiled from public sources that are credited inside each tool. They can contain errors, gaps or outdated values, and they may change when sources are updated.
  • Prices in the Market Atlas are indicative, taken from the dated source shown, and can differ from prices in any particular market on any particular day. They are not an offer, a quote, or trading or investment advice.
  • Flood and weather information is a summary of public forecasts and is not an official warning. Always follow the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (NIHSA), the Nigerian Meteorological Agency (NiMet), NEMA, your State Emergency Management Agency and local authorities.
  • Energy, water, forest and market figures are not certified statistics and are not official figures of the National Bureau of Statistics or any agency, unless a value is labelled with that agency as its source.

5. Peoples, cultures and heritage

Information on peoples, languages, festivals, traditional rulers and heritage is indicative and summarised from the sources cited. Homelands and language areas overlap and are drawn only roughly. Nothing in the atlas takes a position on the origin, ownership of land, chieftaincy, or status of any group, and no group is ranked above another. Communities are welcome to send corrections, preferred names or spellings.

6. Sensitive locations

The atlases show only information that is already public. They are not designed to locate military or security installations (any mention comes from a cited public list, such as a flood-risk community list or an agency register), and infrastructure such as power plants, refineries and pipelines is marked only at approximate site level. If you believe any location should not be shown for safety or security reasons, please contact me and it will be generalised or removed while I review it.
